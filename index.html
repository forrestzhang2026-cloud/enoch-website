About Us

A Ningbo Forwarder Focused on the US Trade Lane

Ningbo Enoch Global Forwarding Co., Ltd was founded in 2021 in Ningbo, China. We are an NVOCC registered with the US Federal Maritime Commission (FMC), specializing in cross-border logistics to the United States for importers, brands and e-commerce sellers. Our team brings 20 years of experience on the China–US trade lane.

Our Ningbo team handles booking, cargo collection and export customs in China, and works with partner agents for shipments from Southeast Asia. In the US, long-term partner agents handle customs clearance, trucking and final delivery. You deal with one team from factory to door.

We work on the principles of honesty, professionalism and fast response: clear quotes, regular updates and a real person who answers your questions.

What We Do

China to USA Logistics Services

Every link from the factory in China or Southeast Asia to the door in the USA, handled by one accountable team.

Ocean Freight to the USA

FCL and LCL from Ningbo, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Qingdao, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia and Cambodia to US West Coast, East Coast and inland points.

Air Freight

For urgent cargo: airport-to-airport or door-to-door to major US cities, priced by time and budget.

US Customs Clearance

ISF filing, customs entry and duty payment through licensed US brokers, with HTS code checks before shipping.

DDP Door-to-Door

Duties and taxes included. We pick up in China and deliver to your US address with one all-in price.

Amazon FBA Delivery

Shipping to Amazon US fulfillment centers, with carton labeling checks and delivery appointments.

US Trucking & Warehousing

Container drayage, LTL/FTL trucking, short-term storage and transloading through our US partners.

How It Works

From Factory to Door in the USA

One contact person follows your shipment through every step and keeps you updated.

  1. Quote & Booking

    Send us cargo details. We compare routes and carriers and confirm an itemized quote.

  2. Pickup at Origin

    We collect from your supplier in China or Southeast Asia, check cartons and labels, and load at the port.

  3. Export & Transit

    Export customs, then ocean or air transport with milestone updates.

  4. US Customs

    ISF, customs entry and duties handled through licensed US brokers.

  5. Final Delivery

    Trucking to your warehouse, store, Amazon FBA center or customer.
